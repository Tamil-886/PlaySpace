About the Ninja Warrior Course

Channel your inner ninja across our triple-lane obstacle gauntlet! The Ninja Warrior Course is specifically engineered for active kids and energetic teens who want to test their grit, balance, and agility. Featuring suspended ring swings, horizontal cargo traverses, rolling balance logs, and the iconic dual curved warped walls (8ft and 10ft), this course brings prime-time television excitement right to Adventure City.

The entire course is suspended directly over a 4-foot deep bed of zero-shock foam cubes and high-density safety mats, guaranteeing that any slip or fall results in an ultra-cushioned, giggly soft landing.

VENUE POLICIES

Recommended Age & Requirements

To ensure safe, exhilarating play for everyone, PlaySpace strictly enforces zone-specific safety boundaries, age brackets, and supervisor-to-child ratios.

Official Age Suitability
👶 Ages 6 - 15 Years (Min 44" Height)
Age Limit: 6 Years old. Participants under 6 are encouraged to enjoy the Toddler Trail or Little Explorers Zone.
Height / Weight: Minimum height: 44 inches (112 cm). Maximum weight: 220 lbs (100 kg).
Supervision: Dedicated Ninja Marshals oversee each run. Spectator viewing platforms line both sides.
Required Gear: Grip socks mandatory. Athletic attire recommended; no loose hanging cords or jewelry.
🛡️

Zone Safety Guidelines

  • 1. Strictly 1 participant per obstacle lane at any time — wait for the lane ahead to be completely clear.
  • 2. If you lose grip, cross your arms across your chest and fall relaxed onto the safety foam landing bed.
  • 3. Never push, shove, or pull on fellow competitors while traversing suspended obstacles.
  • 4. Do not attempt to jump down backwards from the top of the warped wall — use the padded side exit staircase.
  • 5. Follow designated directional arrows from the starting platform to the finish platform only.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Ninja Warrior Course FAQs

Common questions about visiting, gear, and booking this attraction zone.

Q. Is the Ninja Warrior Course safe for beginner climbers?

Absolutely! We feature three clearly marked lanes ranging from beginner green steps to advanced black grips, all cushioned by deep foam pits.

Q. Can kids time their ninja runs?

Yes! Each lane has start and stop digital sensor buzzers that display live times on our arena leaderboard.

Q. Do kids need climbing shoes?

No, standard PlaySpace non-slip grip socks or clean athletic shoes with non-marking rubber soles are perfect.
