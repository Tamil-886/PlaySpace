About the Volcano Climb

Get ready for a fiery adventure on the Volcano Climb! Styled like a glowing neon volcano with bright orange and red magma graphics, this soft-play mountain challenges kids to scale its 50-degree padded cone slope using knotted marine climbing ropes and textured climbing cleats.

Once climbers reach the volcano's summit rim, they can look down into the 'crater' and zoom down an exciting enclosed tube slide straight into a cushioned landing bay of orange and red foam blocks.

VENUE POLICIES

Recommended Age & Requirements

To ensure safe, exhilarating play for everyone, PlaySpace strictly enforces zone-specific safety boundaries, age brackets, and supervisor-to-child ratios.

Official Age Suitability
👶 Ages 3 - 10 Years
Age Limit: 3 Years old (Preschoolers can climb with parent assistance on the lower slope).
Height / Weight: Max climber weight: 180 lbs (82 kg).
Supervision: Staff marshal stationed adjacent to the volcano monitors queue and slide clearance.
Required Gear: Grip socks mandatory to ensure firm footing on the vinyl slope.
🛡️

Zone Safety Guidelines

  • 1. Use climbing ropes with two hands while ascending the volcano slope.
  • 2. Never jump or dive off the volcano cone slope — always use the summit slide to descend.
  • 3. Strictly 2 climbers ascending the slope simultaneously; wait your turn at the base.
  • 4. Slide feet-first only into the crater foam pit.
  • 5. Clear the crater landing pit immediately after arriving at the bottom.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Volcano Climb FAQs

Common questions about visiting, gear, and booking this attraction zone.

Q. Is the slope slippery?

The vinyl surface is textured, and PlaySpace rubber grip socks provide exceptional traction.

Q. What happens if a child slips on the slope?

The entire volcano cone is padded with 3 inches of high-density foam, so slipping simply results in sliding gently down to the bottom mat.

Q. Can a 4-year-old climb it?

Yes! Many 3- and 4-year-olds climb to the summit using the knotted ropes and love the crater slide.
