About the Mega Trampoline Arena

The Mega Trampoline Arena is the pounding heartbeat of PlaySpace — a colossal 14,000-square-foot matrix of seamlessly welded steel frames and high-tensile Olympic springbeds. Designed in strict compliance with ASTM F2970 safety standards, jumpers can seamlessly transition from flat runways to 45-degree angled rebound walls that let you bounce off the perimeter like an acrobatic superhero.

Whether you are practicing rhythmic tuck-jumps, performing synchronized aerial routines with friends, or simply letting boundless energy bounce away, our arena provides unparalleled kinetic rebound with 2-inch cross-linked polyethylene safety padding across all structural steel dividers.

VENUE POLICIES

Recommended Age & Requirements

To ensure safe, exhilarating play for everyone, PlaySpace strictly enforces zone-specific safety boundaries, age brackets, and supervisor-to-child ratios.

Official Age Suitability
👶 Ages 4+ (All Ages & Adults Welcome)
Age Limit: 4 Years (Jumpers 4–6 must have an active adult jumping or observing on court)
Height / Weight: Max weight limit 260 lbs (118 kg). No minimum height requirement.
Supervision: 1 adult observer per 2 young children recommended. Court marshals provide active supervision.
Required Gear: Mandatory PlaySpace rubber-tread grip socks required for all jumpers.
🛡️

Zone Safety Guidelines

  • 1. Strictly 1 jumper per trampoline mat at any time — double bouncing is strictly prohibited.
  • 2. Always land on two feet or seated seat-drop position with arms tucked across your chest.
  • 3. Never attempt flips, inverted tricks, or dives head-first without prior marshal certification.
  • 4. Pockets must be completely emptied into security lockers; no keys, phones, or jewelry permitted on court.
  • 5. Rest immediately along padded perimeter walkways if feeling fatigued or lightheaded.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Mega Trampoline Arena FAQs

Common questions about visiting, gear, and booking this attraction zone.

Q. Can adults jump in the Mega Trampoline Arena with their kids?

Yes! Our commercial trampoline beds are engineered to support adult jumpers up to 260 lbs. We actively encourage parents to jump alongside their children!

Q. Do I have to buy PlaySpace grip socks?

Yes. For hygiene and traction safety, approved non-slip grip socks ($3/pair, reusable) are mandatory for all participants.

Q. Is there a time limit on the trampoline arena?

Your play pass grants all-inclusive access to the trampoline arena for your entire booked duration (60, 90, or 120 minutes).
