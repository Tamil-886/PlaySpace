About the Interactive Play Arena

Step into the future of active entertainment where video games meet physical jump fitness! The Interactive Play Arena features cutting-edge ValoJump augmented reality trampoline screens and interactive motion projection floors. When jumping on our specialized AR trampoline, high-speed camera sensors track your physical body and insert you directly onto the digital game screen as the playable hero.

Battle alien invasions, bounce through floating cloud platforms, dodge virtual fireballs, or challenge a friend to live two-player split-screen air duels! Combining energetic physical jumping with digital gameplay, kids get a full workout without even realizing they are exercising.

VENUE POLICIES

Recommended Age & Requirements

To ensure safe, exhilarating play for everyone, PlaySpace strictly enforces zone-specific safety boundaries, age brackets, and supervisor-to-child ratios.

Official Age Suitability
👶 Ages 3+ (All Ages & Adults)
Age Limit: 3 Years old (Games feature tailored difficulty modes for toddlers, kids, teens, and adults).
Height / Weight: Max jumper weight: 260 lbs (118 kg). No height restrictions.
Supervision: Game kiosks are self-service with staff marshals assisting with game selection and queuing.
Required Gear: Grip socks mandatory. All items removed from pockets.
🛡️

Zone Safety Guidelines

  • 1. Strictly 1 jumper on the ValoJump trampoline mat while the game is active.
  • 2. Jump within the center perimeter markers of the mat to ensure motion sensors track accurately.
  • 3. Soft foam balls only for the interactive smash-wall — never throw shoes, water bottles, or hard objects.
  • 4. Do not touch or lean against the digital projection screen surfaces.
  • 5. Take turns courteously and limit solo sessions to 2 game rounds when others are waiting.

Interactive Play Arena FAQs

Common questions about visiting, gear, and booking this attraction zone.

Q. Can we save videos of our gameplay?

Yes! ValoJump automatically records your video clip, which you can send straight to your email from our kiosk.

Yes! There are gentle modes like 'Toy Stomp' and 'Bubble Pop' designed specifically for young preschoolers.

No! Access to the Interactive Play Arena is 100% included with any standard PlaySpace pass.
